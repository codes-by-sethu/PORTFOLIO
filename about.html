PROFILE / 01
The point of view / 01

Technology is the tool.
The outcome is the point.

Business context first. The right questions next. AI where it creates real value. The work is not about using technology for its own sake — it is about identifying the opportunity and making the solution useful. Business problems rarely arrive as neatly defined technical requirements. They sit within processes, competing priorities, fragmented information and decisions that need to be made. My approach begins with understanding that bigger picture. Grounded in business analysis, computer science and data science, I look at how a business operates, where the gaps are, what the data reveals and where AI can open up a better way forward. With hands-on experience across AI and GenAI, I can take that understanding further — translating business needs into technical requirements, exploring solutions, building prototypes and evaluating their outputs. I bring both the perspective to recognise a meaningful business opportunity and the technical foundation to explore how it can become reality.
